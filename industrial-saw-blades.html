SAFE OPERATION

Verify before operating a saw blade

01 — Compatibility

Match OD, centre bore, pinholes, rotation direction, body thickness, tooth geometry and the blade speed rating to the machine manual.

02 — Inspection

Inspect for cracks, missing tips, deformation and abnormal wear. Never fit a damaged saw blade.

03 — Installation

Isolate machine power and follow flange, clamping and guarding instructions. Do not modify the bore or blade without approved engineering procedures.

04 — Operation

Use the machine guard and required PPE, secure the workpiece, and stop the machine if abnormal vibration or noise occurs.

Always follow the actual machine manufacturer's instructions and applicable workplace safety requirements.
