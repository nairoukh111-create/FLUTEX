About FLUTEX

A focused industrial supply brand.

FLUTEX is built around products that are consumed directly in manufacturing—tools and consumables where the correct specification affects output, quality and continuity.

Our approach

Technical enough to understand the requirement. Commercial enough to supply it clearly.

FLUTEX Industrial focuses on cutting tools, plasma consumables, welding consumables, production fluids and tooling accessories.

The objective is simple: make industrial purchasing clearer by matching the requested product to the actual machine, material and application before quotation.

Application-led

We start with how the product will be used, not only what it is called.

Specification clarity

Dimensions, references and descriptions should be clear enough for purchasing and production teams to verify.

Focused portfolio

We concentrate on recurring industrial tooling and consumables instead of becoming a generic everything catalogue.

Long-term supply mindset

The goal is repeatable supply for production environments, not a one-off one-off transaction.

Corporate structure

UK registered. Regionally operated.

FLUTEX combines a registered UK company structure with regional operators supporting customers across the Middle East and Türkiye.
