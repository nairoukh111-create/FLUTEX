Ball transfer unit selection: load, mounting and spacing

A ball diameter alone does not define a transfer-table replacement. Describe the load, the mounting and how the unit will operate so FLUTEX can review the offered unit against the application.

In this guide

  1. Describe the load throughout its movement
  2. Confirm orientation before using a load rating
  3. Measure the mounting interface
  4. Distinguish the materials and working conditions
  5. Can a similar-looking unit be substituted?

Published: 14 November 2026

Describe the load throughout its movement

Record maximum item mass, the smallest supported footprint, underside material/condition and direction of travel. Include entry and exit transitions and any off-centre loading. Send the table layout with unit spacing and a sketch of the smallest item.

Do not divide the load by every unit installed in the table and assume equal sharing. Uneven surfaces and mounting tolerances can concentrate the load on fewer units. Spacing and capacity must be checked together for the selected design.

Confirm orientation before using a load rating

State whether the ball faces up, down or sideways. An upright rating must not automatically be used for an inverted installation. Ask for the documented capacity in the actual orientation and conditions of use.

This guide does not apply a universal spacing ratio or safety factor. Use the selected unit’s technical data and a review of the actual support arrangement, including unevenness and impact, before approving a table design.

Measure the mounting interface

FieldWhat to provide
Ball and housingLoad-ball diameter, housing diameter, overall height and installed ball projection above the table.
FixingFlange, threaded stud, internal thread or push-fit arrangement; hole centres, thread and retaining details.
TablePlate thickness, mounting-hole size, support beneath the housing and underside installation access.
EnvironmentTemperature, dust/chips, moisture, washdown or chemicals, plus any required corrosion resistance.

Photograph the top, side and underside of the removed unit beside a scale. Give units and tolerances on a controlled drawing where fit is critical.

Distinguish the materials and working conditions

Specify the load ball, supporting balls and housing separately; a stainless load ball does not identify the material of every component. Describe whether surface marking is acceptable and identify the load’s contact surface.

  • State expected travel speed, movement frequency and any shock loading.
  • Describe contamination and the cleaning method.
  • Request documented capacity, material construction and maintenance requirements for the offered unit.
  • Send quantities, GCC country/city and the requested delivery date with the FLUTEX enquiry.

Can a similar-looking unit be substituted?

Only after checking mounting dimensions, installed projection, load capacity in the intended orientation and the application conditions. Matching the ball diameter or number of fixing holes alone does not establish interchangeability.
