BUYER QUESTIONS

Ordering and compatibility.

Are these items kept in stock?

Listed items are RFQ catalog references, not a live stock commitment. Availability, specifications and lead time are confirmed individually.

How do I confirm a protective window?

Share the optical diameter, thickness, material or coating requirement and installed head model; equal dimensions alone do not prove optical equivalence.

Do you supply non-listed references?

Yes. Send the manufacturer reference, drawing, photo and required quantity so FLUTEX can review the requested specification and prepare a quotation.

Prepare a laser consumables and head spare parts RFQ for the GCC

FLUTEX handles technical enquiries for Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman. Include the following product details with the destination.

  • Select the component family: nozzle, protective window, focusing optics, ceramic or head part.
  • Provide the cutting or welding head model and the existing reference where available.
  • For optics, state diameter and thickness; for nozzles and ceramics, add thread and mounting dimensions.
  • Send a clear photo or drawing, quantity and GCC destination. FLUTEX confirms the exact match in its quotation.
