Privacy Notice

How FLUTEX handles information submitted through this website.

Information we receive

When you send an enquiry or RFQ, we may receive your name, company, business contact details, product requirements and any files you choose to attach, such as drawings, PDFs or images.

How we use it

We use submitted information to identify products, prepare quotations, respond to enquiries, coordinate supply and maintain relevant business correspondence.

Website security

This site may use Cloudflare security services, including Turnstile on the RFQ form. Cloudflare may process technical information required to operate and protect the service.

Sharing

FLUTEX does not sell enquiry data. Information may be handled by our operating entities and service providers only as needed to respond to the request, operate the website or deliver the requested business service.

Retention and requests

Business enquiry records are retained only for legitimate operational, legal and commercial needs. For a privacy request, contact sales@flutexindustrial.com.

This website notice describes the current website workflow and should be reviewed if analytics, marketing cookies or additional data services are added.
