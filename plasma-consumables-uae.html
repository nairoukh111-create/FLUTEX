UAE BUYER CHECKLIST

What UAE industrial buyers should include

01

Model and process

Send a machine nameplate photo when the model or torch series is uncertain, plus amperage and cutting process.

02

Part-by-part reference list

List each reference and quantity separately and identify whether retaining caps or swirl rings are needed alongside nozzles and electrodes.

03

Delivery destination and terms

Specify Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah or another emirate. Stock, shipping method, invoice and delivery conditions are checked separately for every quotation.

This page accepts UAE business enquiries; it does not claim a UAE branch, local warehouse, authorized OEM distribution or guaranteed lead time. Compatibility is subject to technical verification.

Frequently asked procurement questions

Are parts interchangeable at the same amperage?

Not necessarily. Original part number, torch configuration and system generation take precedence over amperage alone.

Are UAE prices and stock shown online?

No live UAE stock or prices are promised here. Availability, quoted price and delivery terms are confirmed in the RFQ response.
