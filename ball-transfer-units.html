There is no single universal ISO classification for these names. The families below are common engineering descriptions, not manufacturer-specific series such as CY-A, SP or Omniflow.
Engineering classification

Mounting & fixing styles

Common mounting methods

Flange-Mounted

Round, rectangular or square flange; often two or four fixing holes.

Base-Fixing / Bolt-Down

Secured through a base plate or lower housing fixing holes.

Threaded Stud / Stem Mounted

Integrated threaded stem, stud or bolt for machine installation.

Press-Fit / Drop-In

Designed to seat in a machined hole; verify fit tolerance and shoulder size.

Clamped / Retained

Positioned in a holder, clamp or retaining plate.

Engineering classification

Load & operating design

Select by load case and motion requirements

Light-Duty

For lighter industrial handling applications; check the specific manufacturer rating.

Medium-Duty

General-purpose use with the required number and spacing of support units.

Heavy-Duty

Higher-rated handling for machinery, die movement and transfer decks.

Spring-Loaded

Integrated spring travel for positioning or controlled engagement.

Miniature & Low-Profile

Smaller footprint or reduced installed height.

Engineering classification

Materials & environment

Options that can overlap the mounting types

Carbon Steel

Steel housing or load ball, with finish appropriate to the application.

Stainless Steel

Stainless housing and/or balls for greater corrosion resistance.

Nylon / Polymer Load Ball

Non-metallic ball for applications sensitive to surface marking.

Sealed / Dirt-Resistant

Seal or debris-exit features for dusty or contaminated environments.
