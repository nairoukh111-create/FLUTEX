Industrial cutting oil applications · Process-specific buying guide

CNC cutting oil applications — from gear cutting to precision turning

FLUTEX ESTERION 35 is a Swiss-origin, neat synthetic-ester cutting oil offered for evaluation in MQL and oil-flood metalworking. The machining operations below help industrial buyers identify relevant cutting-fluid applications and equipment categories; machine compatibility and process settings must be confirmed individually.

5-Axis CNC aluminium profile machining

For 5-axis CNC machining centres and aluminium-profile cutting, review the profile alloy, tool engagement and MQL or oil-flood delivery. ESTERION 35 has achieved excellent results in actual 5-axis CNC aluminium machining applications; those results do not establish blanket approval for every machine.

Gear hobbing, gear shaping, power skiving and gear milling

Application families include CNC gear hobbing machines, gear shaping machines, power skiving machines and gear milling machines. For gear tooth machining, specify the steel grade, cutting tool and oil-delivery system when requesting a gear-cutting fluid recommendation.

CNC milling, face milling, profile milling, slot milling, pocket milling and thread milling

Review CNC milling oil for vertical machining centres (VMC), horizontal machining centres (HMC), and 3-axis, 4-axis or 5-axis machines. Face, profile, slot and pocket milling are sub-operations of CNC milling; thread milling requires an application-specific tooling and lubrication check.

CNC turning, Swiss-type precision turning and turn-mill machining

For CNC lathes, turning centres, Swiss-type CNC lathes and combined turn-mill machining centres, match cutting-oil selection to the workpiece material, cutting operation and lubrication arrangement. Swiss-type and turn-mill configurations need individual review rather than automatic approval.

Broaching, cold sawing, reaming and thread cutting

Industrial applications include broaching machines, circular cold saws, reaming operations and thread cutting on CNC lathes or threading equipment. State the cutting-tool geometry, workpiece and oil-flow system so these processes can be assessed accurately.

Machining-process coverage describes application families, not a guarantee of suitability for every tool, workpiece grade, machine model or MQL atomizer. For drilling, deep-hole drilling, counterboring, gear shaving, slotting and punching, see the separate application-review section below.

Lubrication system selection

Neat MQL oil and flood lubrication — no water dilution

ESTERION 35 is an ester-rich cutting oil with a nominal viscosity of 35 cSt at 40°C and a typical open-cup flash point of 310°C. Use it neat (undiluted) with compatible MQL or oil-flood equipment. Confirm dosing, spray delivery, seals, filtration, pump suitability, ventilation and the machine maker’s requirements before changing fluids. A high flash point is not a substitute for adequate mist control and fire precautions.

Technical buyer answers

Frequently asked questions about FLUTEX ESTERION 35

Is ESTERION 35 suitable for 5-axis CNC machining?

The oil has delivered excellent results in actual aluminium machining applications on 5-axis CNC centres. Confirm the machine’s MQL or oil-flood system and processing conditions before use.

Is ESTERION 35 a water-miscible coolant?

No. It is a neat synthetic ester cutting oil used undiluted in correctly configured MQL or flood lubrication equipment. Do not add water.

Which machining processes are covered by the FLUTEX technical data?

The technical data lists gear hobbing, gear shaping, gear skiving, broaching, milling, turning, reaming, threading and cold sawing.

Is drilling, gear shaving or punching already approved for this oil?

These operations are not listed as approved applications in the current FLUTEX product data sheet. Request an application review and controlled trial before use.
