FLUTEX FERRUM EP 23

Chlorine-free, sulfurized extreme-pressure neat cutting oil for demanding machining of ferrous metals and difficult-to-machine alloys.

Product overview

Extreme-pressure lubricity supports heavy cuts and demanding machining while assisting workpiece surface finish and cutting tool life.

Materials & machining operations

Materials: Ferrous metals, alloy steels and hard-to-machine ferrous alloys. Contains active sulfur; compatibility with aluminium, copper, brass and other yellow/white metals must be tested before use.

Operations: Threading, tapping, broaching, high-speed machining and high metal-removal operations.

Application, storage & safety

Use neat (undiluted). Contains active sulfur. Do not assume compatibility with aluminium, copper or brass without testing. Documented packaging formats: 20 L HDPE pail and 208 L drum. Follow the current Safety Data Sheet.
