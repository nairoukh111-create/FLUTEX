ESAB compatible consumables — frequently asked questions

Which ESAB plasma systems does FLUTEX cover?

FLUTEX covers 3 ESAB system families: PT-36, PT-19XLS and PT-600. Each system has its own page listing the original reference numbers covered.

How many original ESAB reference numbers are covered?

Approximately 135 original ESAB reference numbers are covered across these systems, spanning diffuser, electrode, electrode holder, gas baffle, nozzle, nozzle base, nozzle tip, o-ring, retainer cup, shield, torch body and torch sleeve. Brand and part-number references identify compatibility only and are not sold as genuine OEM parts unless stated in the quotation.

Does FLUTEX supply ESAB compatible consumables in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. FLUTEX Middle East operations are supported from Riyadh and serve industrial buyers across Saudi Arabia, the wider Gulf and Türkiye. Send the ESAB reference number, system model and quantity for a quotation.
