Thermal Dynamics compatible consumables — frequently asked questions

Which Thermal Dynamics plasma systems does FLUTEX cover?

FLUTEX covers 4 Thermal Dynamics system families: SL60 / SL100, SL40, PCH/M-62 / PCH/M-102 and XT301. Each system has its own page listing the original reference numbers covered.

How many original Thermal Dynamics reference numbers are covered?

Approximately 224 original Thermal Dynamics reference numbers are covered across these systems, spanning electrode, gas distributor, o-ring, shield cap, shield cup, standoff guide, start cartridge, swirl ring and tip. Brand and part-number references identify compatibility only and are not sold as genuine OEM parts unless stated in the quotation.

Does FLUTEX supply Thermal Dynamics compatible consumables in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. FLUTEX Middle East operations are supported from Riyadh and serve industrial buyers across Saudi Arabia, the wider Gulf and Türkiye. Send the Thermal Dynamics reference number, system model and quantity for a quotation.
