Cebora compatible consumables — frequently asked questions

Which Cebora plasma systems does FLUTEX cover?

FLUTEX covers 5 Cebora system families: CP200, CP161, CP162C, CP251 / CP251G and CP450 / CP450G. Each system has its own page listing the original reference numbers covered.

How many original Cebora reference numbers are covered?

Approximately 163 original Cebora reference numbers are covered across these systems, spanning electrode, nozzle, retaining cap, shield, shield cap, spacer, spring, swirl ring, torch head and water tube. Brand and part-number references identify compatibility only and are not sold as genuine OEM parts unless stated in the quotation.
