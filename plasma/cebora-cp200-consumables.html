CP200 consumables — frequently asked questions

Which consumables does FLUTEX supply for the Cebora CP200?

FLUTEX supplies compatible spacer, spring, shield cap, retaining cap, nozzle, swirl ring, electrode, water tube and torch head for the Cebora CP family, covering machine torch and cutting configurations. Compatible CP200 replacement consumables.

Are the original Cebora part numbers for the CP200 cross-referenced?

Yes. This range covers 21 original Cebora reference numbers for the CP200, including 1367, 1386, 1619, 1760 and others listed on this page. Send the original reference and FLUTEX confirms the compatible replacement in writing before quotation. Brand and part-number references are used only to identify compatibility and are not offered as genuine OEM parts unless stated in the quotation.

What information is needed to quote CP200 consumables?

Send the original reference number, the system model (CP200), the operating amperage, the process (machine torch or cutting) and the required quantity. That is enough for FLUTEX to match the part and return price and lead time.
