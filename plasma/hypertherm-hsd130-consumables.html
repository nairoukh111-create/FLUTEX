HSD130 consumables — frequently asked questions

Which consumables does FLUTEX supply for the Hypertherm HSD130?

FLUTEX supplies compatible shield, retaining cap, nozzle, electrode, swirl ring and water tube for the Hypertherm HSD family, covering machine torch, mild steel, oxygen and air configurations. Compatible consumables for HSD130 mild-steel plasma cutting configurations.

Are the original Hypertherm part numbers for the HSD130 cross-referenced?

Yes. This range covers 72 original Hypertherm reference numbers for the HSD130, including 220179, 220180, 220181, 220182 and others listed on this page. Send the original reference and FLUTEX confirms the compatible replacement in writing before quotation. Brand and part-number references are used only to identify compatibility and are not offered as genuine OEM parts unless stated in the quotation.

What information is needed to quote HSD130 consumables?

Send the original reference number, the system model (HSD130), the operating amperage, the process (machine torch, mild steel, oxygen or air) and the required quantity. That is enough for FLUTEX to match the part and return price and lead time.

Can FLUTEX supply HSD130 plasma consumables in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. FLUTEX Middle East operations are supported from Riyadh and serve industrial buyers across Saudi Arabia, including Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Jubail, as well as the wider Gulf and Türkiye. Quantity, price, availability and lead time for Hypertherm HSD130 consumables are confirmed per RFQ.
