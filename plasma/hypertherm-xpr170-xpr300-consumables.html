XPR170 / XPR300 consumables — frequently asked questions

Which consumables does FLUTEX supply for the Hypertherm XPR170 / XPR300?

FLUTEX supplies compatible shield cap, shield, retaining cap, nozzle, electrode, swirl ring, water tube and torch parts for the Hypertherm XPR family, covering machine torch, mild steel, stainless steel and non-ferrous configurations. Compatible replacement consumables for XPR170 and XPR300 cutting systems.

Are the original Hypertherm part numbers for the XPR170 / XPR300 cross-referenced?

Yes. This range covers 72 original Hypertherm reference numbers for the XPR170 / XPR300, including 020039, 020042, 020392, 020579 and others listed on this page. Send the original reference and FLUTEX confirms the compatible replacement in writing before quotation. Brand and part-number references are used only to identify compatibility and are not offered as genuine OEM parts unless stated in the quotation.

What information is needed to quote XPR170 / XPR300 consumables?

Send the original reference number, the system model (XPR170 / XPR300), the operating amperage, the process (machine torch, mild steel, stainless steel or non-ferrous) and the required quantity. That is enough for FLUTEX to match the part and return price and lead time.

Can FLUTEX supply XPR170 / XPR300 plasma consumables in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. FLUTEX Middle East operations are supported from Riyadh and serve industrial buyers across Saudi Arabia, including Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Jubail, as well as the wider Gulf and Türkiye. Quantity, price, availability and lead time for Hypertherm XPR170 / XPR300 consumables are confirmed per RFQ.
