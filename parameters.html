How to use the calculation

Use the effective diameter at the cutting point. It may differ from nominal diameter for ball-nose or tapered tools. Confirm spindle and holder limits, lubrication, axial depth and radial engagement before machining.

For aluminum 6063, include the alloy, operation, exact tool, machine and lubrication equipment when requesting cutting data. Alloy designation alone does not define suitable operating values.
